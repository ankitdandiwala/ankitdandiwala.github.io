01 / PROFILE

Profile

My work has covered vehicle dynamics modelling, multibody simulation, suspension engineering, system identification and control. My doctoral research at IIT Kharagpur focused on active tilt and rollover stability control of narrow electric three-wheelers.

In industry, I have worked on suspension kinematics and multibody modelling, four-wheel vehicle-dynamics modelling, data-based model correction, steering and braking system identification, and autonomous-vehicle platform development and integration.
